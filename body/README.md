# Body

*Early-stage app idea — not being built yet. Kept here so the thread isn't lost between conversations.*

## What it is

An app for the body, from ideas Nate has held for a long time. It would bring together exercises from David Grey, Shah Boroumand, and others. The rest of the ideas are still to be captured here, in his words.

## How it relates to the rest

Nate doesn't know yet whether there is reason to integrate it with `life/`. The plan is to build it independently first, as `life/` and `finance/` are, using the shared `js/github.js`, and to add functions to `js/api.js` only if the two ever need to share data.

## One app or two

Nate's idea (September 2026): design body and mind as two layers, one for organizing exercises and one for the exercises themselves as data, and perhaps a single app serves both, so others can bring their own ideas. The full sketch is in `mind/README.md`.

## The ideas

- There are at least three bodies Nate wants moving better: his own, and both of his kids'.
- A software alternative to consulting a movement coach like David Grey: guide a person through the range of possible movements they might want to explore, rather than requiring them to already know what to ask for.

## Open questions

- Where does its data live: `life-management-data`, or a private repository of its own the way finance has one?
- Is it one app together with `mind/`, or two?
