What we've built

Inbox by area

Messages grouped by life area (Finance, Kids & school, Health, Travel…). Scan & delete for marketing clutter. Read what matters.

Transactions

Life parses Citi charges from email into a transaction inbox. Finances imports and stores in personal-financial-data. Event purchases become Schedule tasks.

  • Event charges move out of Transactions into Schedule tasks
  • Recurring identification (GEICO, subscriptions) happens in Finances after import
  • Process in Finances → stores recurring keys, surfaces hints

Finance tasks

Annual Account Summary emails → one-click task to retrieve the summary. Tasks live in GitHub, shared across sessions.

Merchant knowledge

Merchant knowledge in finance/config/merchants.json — recurring flag, category, hints. Life consumes from Finances if needed.

What we're building

First-time vs recurring

First-time transactions are more concerning — they need attention. Recurring charges that meet expectations could go straight to the finance repository, available for finance views but excluded from handling inbound email (no inbox work).

Recurring + budget

Compare recurring charges to expectations. Alert on changes (price increase, new recurring, expected charge missing).

Event ↔ calendar

Connect event purchases to GoFan/Eventbrite confirmation emails and calendar. "Do I have this event scheduled?"

Finance guidance

Suggest ways to reduce recurring costs (coverage on older vehicles, subscription audits).
